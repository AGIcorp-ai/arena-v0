Why this architecture compounds

1) Competition creates pressure
Battle royale forces adaptation. Weak behaviors get punished; robust behaviors survive.
2) Everything is auditable
Append-only, hash-chained event log plus full artifacts. You can replay and inspect why something improved.
3) Learning is modular
Skills are compact, composable, and can be applied immediately as policy. Later: real finetunes via adapter tokens.
4) Deterministic replay
Proxy state transitions are reproducible from seed + logged events, making claims testable.
